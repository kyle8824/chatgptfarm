# Cold-weather preparation

Cold villagers can now work toward protection that does not yet exist. The old basic shelter required four dry branches, while fuel gathering stopped at two wet branches and the new camps had no shelter in which to dry them. This created a circular prerequisite and left fully rested people choosing idle breaks.

The existing crude branch shelter accepts four raw branches, wet or dry. Construction still takes 180 simulated minutes and consumes conserved wood into shelter components; it creates no heat. Newly completed shelters expose an empty shared drying pile at the physical shelter. Cold preparation gathers or retrieves enough finite local material, preserves that material through cargo handling, and carries fuel into covered storage. Two drying branches form a reserve; ordinary gathering and optional practice cannot repeatedly consume that last reserve. Additional known supplies remain usable for tools and open construction projects.

Preparation receives survival priority over leisure, including interrupting existing quiet breaks. Critical food, water and exhaustion still take precedence. Villagers with four carried branches may walk back to a known empty camp to build; unknown settlements remain unknown and household ancestry does not bind movement. Source lookup, failed-route cooldowns and idle rechecks remain bounded. All gathering, handling and travel use existing physical reach and finite-inventory checks.

There is no artificial warming, world reset, forced task, API-cadence change or quota reset. Wet shelter material is not usable fire fuel merely because it was built with. Drying still follows existing moisture and weather integration.
